# Public research-data release checklist

The original iMotions `raw_data.zip` includes participant demographics, original respondent labels, study identifiers and capture dates/times; those originals are **not** included in the 2026 GitHub-ready package. The released files are derived and pseudonymised (`REC001` etc), containing only selected analysis signals.

The author indicated that publication is permitted. The publisher must independently confirm the following before pushing the files to a genuinely public remote:

- [ ] The original consent/ethics materials or data-sharing approval **explicitly permit public release of participant-level behavioural/physiological recordings**, not merely participation in a study.
- [ ] The institution/research supervisor has not reserved rights in the data or restricted public deposition.
- [ ] The chosen scope of release and data licence do not exceed what the participants and rights holders authorised.
- [ ] The remaining signal data has undergone a re-identification risk assessment appropriate to the audience and use (pseudonymisation alone is not proof of anonymity).
- [ ] The original stimuli and any third-party media are cleared if added later. They are not included by default.
- [ ] A process exists for responding to a data-subject concern and for removing content promptly if needed.

Relevant ICO guidance recognises gaze analysis as a form of biometric processing in certain circumstances, and distinguishes research participation consent from the lawful bases for processing personal data. Whether this dataset falls within a specific legal category depends on its purpose, identifiability and circumstances, not simply its format.
